      About SphereX

      SphereX shows images from NASA's SPHEREx telescope, which maps the whole sky every six months in 102 colours of infrared light. Pick a place to see every image SPHEREx took of it, and watch how it changed over time.

      The colour control steps through SPHEREx's 102 infrared colours, from 0.75 to 5 microns.

      Data: SPHEREx DR1 and QR3 from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA). Sky viewer: Aladin Lite, CDS Strasbourg. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
